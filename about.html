Our Story

Born in J&K, Built for J&K Businesses

REBRAR was founded with one simple mission: to bring world-class web design and development services to the businesses of Jammu & Kashmir. We saw local businesses across Srinagar, Jammu, Baramulla, Anantnag, and hundreds of smaller towns struggling to establish a credible online presence.

We started as a small team of passionate designers and developers from J&K who believed that geography shouldn't limit great design. Today, we've grown into the region's most trusted digital agency, having delivered 150+ websites to clients across every district of J&K — from Kupwara in the north to Kishtwar in the east and Poonch in the west.

Our deep understanding of the local market, combined with our technical expertise in modern web technologies, allows us to create websites that don't just look good — they rank on Google, convert visitors, and grow businesses.
