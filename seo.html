SEO Has Changed. Has Your Website Kept Up?

Search isn't just about ranking on Google's blue links anymore. More people are getting answers directly from Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity. If your website isn't structured to be understood and cited by these systems, you're invisible to a growing share of searches.

REBRAR builds SEO strategy around three layers: traditional SEO (technical health, keywords, local search), AEO — Answer Engine Optimization (structuring content so AI tools can quote and cite you accurately), and GEO — Generative Engine Optimization (being included in AI-generated search summaries). Every website we build gets this foundation from day one.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SEO, AEO, and GEO?

+

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) focuses on ranking in traditional Google search results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) focuses on getting your content cited accurately by AI chat tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) focuses on appearing in AI-generated search summaries like Google's AI Overviews. REBRAR builds strategy across all three.

Will REBRAR optimize my site to appear in ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews?

+

Yes. REBRAR structures content with clear answers, FAQ schema, and well-organized information so AI systems can accurately read, quote, and cite your business.

How long does SEO take to show results?

+

Local SEO improvements can show early signals within 4–8 weeks, but meaningful ranking growth typically takes 3–6 months of consistent optimization, depending on competition.

Do you offer SEO as a standalone service, or only with website builds?

+

SEO is included as a foundation in every website REBRAR builds, and is also available as a standalone service for existing websites that need optimization.

Is local SEO for J&K districts included?

+

Yes, local SEO covering Srinagar, Jammu, and all districts of J&K is a core part of our SEO service for regional businesses.
