Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2025  ·  Effective: January 1, 2025  ·  toolvera.in

This Privacy Policy explains what information Toolvera collects when you use the website at toolvera.in, how that information is used, and what choices you have. We have written this policy in plain language because we believe you deserve to understand it.

By using Toolvera, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you have questions, contact us at privacy@toolvera.in.

1. Files You Upload

Client-Side Tools (Image & Text Tools)

For tools that process files directly in your browser — including our image compressor, format converters, resizer, cropper, grayscale converter, rotate tool, watermark tool, and all text tools — your file is processed using JavaScript running in your own browser tab. The file never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to our servers at any point. We have no access to these files.

Server-Side Tools (PDF Merge, Image to PDF)

For tools that require temporary server-side processing, your file is transmitted securely over HTTPS, processed, and then automatically deleted from our servers immediately after the job completes. We do not retain, review, analyze, index, or share uploaded files. Files are not backed up.

2. Usage Data We Collect

We collect standard web analytics data to understand how the platform is used. This includes: your approximate geographic location (country/region level, derived from IP address), browser type and version, operating system, pages visited and tools used, and session duration. This data is collected in aggregate and is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

3. Contact Form Data

If you use our Contact page, we collect the name, email address, and message text you provide. This information is used only to respond to your inquiry and is never added to a marketing list or shared with any third party.

4. Cookies

Toolvera uses two categories of cookies:

  • Functional cookies: Remember your preferences such as recently used tools.
  • Advertising cookies: Placed by Google AdSense to serve relevant advertisements. These are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling advertising cookies will not affect your ability to use any tool on the platform.

5. Advertising (Google AdSense)

Toolvera is funded by advertising through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your previous visits to our site and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info. The presence of an advertisement on Toolvera does not constitute our endorsement of the advertised product or service.

6. Third-Party Services

We use Google AdSense for advertising and Google Analytics (or similar) for aggregate usage statistics. These services collect data governed by their own privacy policies. We do not use any third-party file processing services — all server-side operations run on Toolvera's own infrastructure.

7. Data Security

All connections between your browser and our servers are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Server-side files are stored in isolated, access-controlled environments during processing and are purged immediately on completion. We apply industry-standard security practices and review them regularly.

8. Children's Privacy

Toolvera is a general-purpose tools platform and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us at privacy@toolvera.in and we will promptly delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you. Since we collect minimal personal data and do not build user profiles, most requests are resolved quickly. Email privacy@toolvera.in with any privacy-related requests.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of Toolvera after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For questions, contact privacy@toolvera.in.
