Know What's Actually Working on Your Website

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need Google Analytics on my website?

+

Yes — without analytics, you have no visibility into how many people visit your site, where they come from, or what they do. It's essential for making informed decisions about your website and marketing.

Is analytics setup included with a new website?

+

Basic Google Analytics and Search Console setup is included with every website REBRAR builds. Advanced conversion tracking is available as part of our analytics service.

Can you set up tracking for WhatsApp clicks and form submissions?

+

Yes, we configure goal and event tracking for WhatsApp clicks, phone calls, form submissions, and bookings so you can measure real conversions, not just visits.

Will I be able to understand the analytics myself?

+

Yes, we set things up clearly and can walk you through your dashboard. Monthly reporting is also available if you'd prefer a simplified summary instead.

What is Google Search Console used for?

+

Search Console shows how your website performs in Google Search — which keywords bring traffic, indexing issues, and mobile usability problems that could affect rankings.
