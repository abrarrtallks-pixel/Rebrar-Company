Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?

+

We offer flexible packages starting from ₹5,000 for basic websites up to ₹50,000+ for advanced ecommerce platforms. Every package includes design, development, SEO optimization, and hosting setup.

How long does website development take?

+

Standard business websites take 7-14 days. Ecommerce sites take 15-25 days. Complex applications may take 30-60 days. We work fast without compromising quality.

Will my website rank on Google?

+

Every REBRAR website is built with SEO in mind. We optimize site structure, speed, mobile responsiveness, keywords, and content. Ranking depends on competition and content quality, but we give you the best foundation.

Do you provide ongoing support?

+

Yes! After launch, we provide 3-6 months of free support including bug fixes, updates, and optimization. Ongoing maintenance packages are available at affordable monthly rates.

Can you build an ecommerce website?

+

Absolutely! We build complete online stores with product catalogs, shopping carts, secure payments, inventory management, and order tracking. We integrate with Razorpay, Stripe, PayPal, and other gateways.

What if I need revisions?

+

We include unlimited revisions during the development process. Your satisfaction is our goal. Once you're happy, we deploy and provide ongoing support.
